Ramesh Pisharody

Television Host • Comedian • Actor

One of Malayalam television’s familiar comedy hosts, Ramesh Pisharody built a strong television identity through comedy, mimicry and celebrity-oriented programmes.

Contribution to Kerala Television

His television journey includes early hosting and comedy appearances, Cinemala and Bluff Masters, followed by Badai Bungalow, where his hosting and comic timing became especially recognisable to Malayalam TV audiences.

Known For

Cinemala • Bluff Masters • Badai Bungalow • Cinema Chirima • Home Minister

Why They Matter

Host of Badai Bungalow; known for combining live-style humour, celebrity interaction and character-based comedy.

Mukesh

Actor • Television Personality • Host

Mukesh is a major Malayalam screen personality who has also been an important presence in television entertainment, particularly comedy and celebrity programmes.

Contribution to Kerala Television

His television work broadened his audience beyond cinema, with Badai Bungalow becoming a notable example of his role as a television personality and performer.

Known For

Badai Bungalow • Asianet Television appearances • Celebrity and comedy programmes

Why They Matter

A bridge between Malayalam cinema and television entertainment, bringing an established film presence into a recurring TV format.

Television Personality

Pearle Maaney

Television Presenter • VJ • Actress

Pearle Maaney became widely recognised as a television presenter and VJ before expanding into acting and digital entertainment.

Contribution to Kerala Television

She anchored Yes Jukebox and hosted programmes including Taste of Kerala and D4 Dance. Her television career also included Cinema Company and her widely followed appearance in Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 1.

Known For

Yes Jukebox • Taste of Kerala • D4 Dance • Cinema Company • Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 1

Why They Matter

Known for a lively presenting style and for moving successfully between television, film and digital media.

Swasika Vijay

Television Actress • Host

Swasika Vijay has built a substantial Malayalam television career through serials, reality programmes and hosting assignments.

Contribution to Kerala Television

Her television breakthrough is strongly associated with the character Seetha, while later work included Manampole Mangalyam, Red Carpet and other entertainment programmes.

Known For

Chinthavishtayaya Seetha • Seetha • Manampole Mangalyam • Red Carpet • Mazhavil Music Awards

A prominent television performer whose work spans fiction, hosting and entertainment programming.

Veena Nair

Television Actress • Performer

Veena Nair is a familiar Malayalam television performer known for comic and character-driven roles.

Contribution to Kerala Television

She became especially recognisable to television audiences through Thatteem Mutteem as Kokila and later appeared in Badai Bungalow, adding a strong comedy presence to both serial and non-fiction television.

Known For

Sthreethwam • Thatteem Mutteem • Badai Bungalow

Why They Matter

Her contribution to Malayalam television is closely linked with memorable character performances and comedy.

Television Personality

Noby Marcose

Comedian • Television Performer • Actor

Noby Marcose rose to prominence through Malayalam television comedy and later carried that popularity into films and reality television.

Contribution to Kerala Television

He became widely known through Comedy Stars and subsequently appeared in programmes including Comedy Utsavam, Tamar Padar, Comedy Stars Season 2 and Star Magic, alongside his film career.

Known For

Comedy Stars • Comedy Utsavam • Tamar Padar • Comedy Masala • Star Magic • Bigg Boss Malayalam 3

Why They Matter

Known for improvisational comedy, ensemble performances and a strong transition from television comedy to cinema.

Anu Joseph

Television Actress • Performer

Anu Joseph is a Malayalam television actress whose career has included long-running serial roles and popular character performances.

Contribution to Kerala Television

She entered the serial world through Chithralekha and went on to appear in programmes including Makalude Amma, Minnukettu, Aalilathaali, Snehachandhrika and Oridathoridathu.

Known For

Chithralekha • Makalude Amma • Minnukettu • Aalilathaali • Snehachandhrika • Oridathoridathu

Why They Matter

A familiar face of Malayalam serial television, with a career built around varied character roles.

Television Personality

Sreekandan Nair

Television Host • Journalist • Producer

Sreekandan Nair is a veteran Malayalam television host and media personality whose career has connected journalism, television production and long-form talk programming.

Contribution to Kerala Television

He became closely associated with programmes such as Oru Nimisham and Nammal Thammil, later establishing Flowers TV and hosting The Sreekandan Nair Show and Flowers Oru Kodi.

Known For

Oru Nimisham • Nammal Thammil • Samadooram • The Sreekandan Nair Show • Flowers Oru Kodi

Why They Matter

A major figure in Malayalam television talk programming, known for long-form interviews, audience interaction and television production.
