The story

Who is Tovino Thomas?

Tovino Thomas is an Indian actor and producer who has become an important figure in contemporary Malayalam cinema. His performances are often associated with an approachable, understated screen presence, while his film choices have taken him across romance, comedy, family drama, action and more experimental material.

After studying engineering and working professionally before entering cinema, he made his acting debut in Vineeth Sreenivasan's Prabhuvinte Makkal (2010). A run of successful films soon established him as one of the most recognisable actors of his generation.

Ennu Ninte Moideen, Guppy, Ohm Shanthi Oshaana, 2018 and especially Minnal Murali helped define his popularity during the 2010s. He subsequently moved into more varied roles, including the critically acclaimed A.R.M, while continuing to work in mainstream Malayalam cinema and selected Tamil projects.

At a glance

  • Born on 21 January 1989 in Irinjalakuda, Kerala.
  • Screen debut in Prabhuvinte Makkal (2010).
  • Breakthrough with Ennu Ninte Moideen (2012).
  • Minnal Murali (2015) became a defining film of his career.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for A.R.M.
  • Founded the production company Tovino Thomas Productions.
  • Continues to work across Malayalam and other Indian-language cinema.
