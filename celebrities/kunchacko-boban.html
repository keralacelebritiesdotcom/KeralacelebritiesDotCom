The story

Who is Kunchacko Boban?

Kunchacko Boban is an Indian actor and film producer who has built one of the more recognisable long-running careers in Malayalam cinema. He first became widely known as a romantic leading man before gradually taking on a broader range of roles.

He made his acting debut opposite Shalini in Fazil's Aniyathipravu (1997). The film became a major success and established him as a prominent young star, with his screen persona quickly earning him the affectionate name Chackochan.

Over the years, his filmography expanded beyond romance into comedy, family drama, crime, thriller and socially rooted stories. Films such as Niram, Traffic, How Old Are You, Take Off, Virus, Nna Thaan Case Kodu and 2018 illustrate the range of his later career.

Beyond acting

A performer with a long cinema legacy

Kunchacko Boban belongs to the Udaya film family and is the grandson of Udaya Pictures founder Kunchacko. His family connection to Malayalam cinema is an important part of his background, although his own career has developed across several distinct phases and screen identities.

He has also been involved in film production, giving him a role in Malayalam cinema beyond acting. His later filmography demonstrates a willingness to work within both commercial formats and more unconventional projects.

Career characteristics

  • Long-running career beginning in the 1990s.
  • Strong early association with romantic cinema.
  • Transition toward mature and character-oriented roles.
  • Regular presence in ensemble films.
  • Experience as both actor and producer.
