Unni Mukundan

Unni Mukundan is an Indian actor, producer and playback singer known for his work in Malayalam cinema and other South Indian films. From his early breakthrough in Mallu Singh to films such as Meppadiyan, Malikappuram and Marco, his career has moved across commercial entertainers, character roles and film production.

The story

Who is Unni Mukundan?

Unnikrishnan Mukundan Nair, professionally known as Unni Mukundan, grew up in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and studied at Pragati Higher Secondary School before completing his graduation in English Literature and Journalism at Prajyoti Niketan College in Thrissur.

Before entering cinema, Mukundan worked in the BPO industry. He made his film debut with the Tamil film Seedan in 2011 and followed it with Bombay March 12. His Malayalam career gathered momentum with films such as Mallu Singh, Vikramadithyan, KL 10 Patthu, Style and Masterpiece.

He later expanded into production through Unni Mukundan Films. Meppadiyan and Shefeekkinte Santhosham were among the company's early productions, while Malikappuram and Marco became important milestones in his career as a leading actor.

Career milestones

From debutant to leading man and producer

1987

Born

Unni Mukundan was born on 22 September 1987 and grew up in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

2011

Seedan and Bombay March 12

He made his film debut with the Tamil film Seedan and also appeared in Bombay March 12, beginning his career in South Indian cinema.

2012

Mallu Singh

The action-comedy Mallu Singh became a significant early Malayalam success and helped establish Mukundan as a leading young actor.

2014–17

Expanding range

Films including Vikramadithyan, KL 10 Patthu, Style, Achayans, Clint and Masterpiece showed different sides of his screen persona. He also began contributing as a lyricist and playback singer.

2016

Telugu debut

He entered Telugu cinema with Janatha Garage, directed by Koratala Siva.

2020–22

Production and Meppadiyan

Unni Mukundan Films was launched in 2020. Meppadiyan (2022) became a major production milestone and won the National Film Award for Best Debut Film of a Director for Vishnu Mohan.

2022

Malikappuram

He played the central role of Ayyappan/Ayyappadas in Malikappuram, which became one of his notable commercial successes.

2024–25

Marco and SIIMA recognition

Marco became a major action-film milestone for Mukundan. In 2025, he won the SIIMA Critics Choice Best Actor award in Malayalam for the film.

Beyond acting

Producer, singer and entrepreneur

Unni Mukundan has expanded his career beyond acting through Unni Mukundan Films, which he launched in 2020. The company produced films including Meppadiyan and Shefeekkinte Santhosham.

He has also contributed to film music as a playback singer and lyricist. His music-related credits include work associated with Achayans, Oru Kuttanadan Blog, Shylock, Vidhi and Shefeekkinte Santhosham.
