Dhyan Sreenivasan

Dhyan Sreenivasan is a Malayalam actor, screenwriter, director and producer known for his comic timing, conversational performances and work across contemporary Malayalam cinema. He is the younger son of actor and screenwriter Sreenivasan and the younger brother of filmmaker and actor Vineeth Sreenivasan.

The story

Who is Dhyan Sreenivasan?

Dhyan made his acting debut in Thira (2013), directed by his brother Vineeth Sreenivasan. The film introduced him in a serious role alongside Shobana and marked the beginning of his career in Malayalam cinema.

He expanded into writing with films such as Adi Kapyare Koottamani and Goodalochana, and made his feature directorial debut with Love Action Drama (2019), starring Nivin Pauly and Nayanthara.

As an actor, Dhyan became particularly recognisable for his relaxed comic performances and conversational interviews, while continuing to move between comedy, family drama and supporting character roles.

Beyond acting

More than an actor

Dhyan Sreenivasan's work extends across multiple areas of Malayalam cinema, allowing him to contribute both in front of and behind the camera.

His career demonstrates the increasingly fluid roles taken by contemporary Malayalam film personalities, where acting, writing, production and direction can overlap.

Why Dhyan Sreenivasan matters

  • His career combines acting, screenwriting and direction.
