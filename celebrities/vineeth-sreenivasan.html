Vineeth Sreenivasan

Vineeth Sreenivasan is a Malayalam actor, filmmaker, singer, lyricist, screenwriter and producer whose career has connected contemporary film music with a distinctive style of youthful storytelling. He is the elder son of actor and screenwriter Sreenivasan.

The story

Who is Vineeth Sreenivasan?

Vineeth began his film career through music and playback singing before establishing himself as an actor and filmmaker. His early association with Malayalam film music helped him build a strong audience among younger viewers.

His directorial debut, Malarvaadi Arts Club (2010), introduced a new ensemble of performers and established his interest in friendship, ambition, music and youthful relationships. He followed it with Thattathin Marayathu, Thira, Jacobinte Swargarajyam and Hridayam.

As an actor, he has appeared in films including Cycle, Traffic, Kunjiramayanam, Aravindante Athidhikal, Hridayam and Mukundan Unni Associates. His work as a singer and lyricist has also remained an important part of his identity.

At a glance

  • Born 1 October 1984 in Thalassery, Kannur.
  • Elder son of actor and screenwriter Sreenivasan.
  • Began his film career as a playback singer.
  • Directed Malarvaadi Arts Club in 2010.
  • Directed Thattathin Marayathu, Thira, Jacobinte Swargarajyam, Hridayam and Varshangalkku Shesham.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Film with Popular Appeal and Aesthetic Value for Hridayam.
  • Also works as an actor, lyricist, screenwriter and producer.

Beyond acting

More than an actor

Vineeth Sreenivasan's work extends across multiple areas of Malayalam cinema, allowing him to contribute both in front of and behind the camera.

His career demonstrates the increasingly fluid roles taken by contemporary Malayalam film personalities, where acting, writing, production and direction can overlap.

Why Vineeth Sreenivasan matters

  • A rare Malayalam film personality who has built parallel careers in singing, acting and direction.
  • His films often combine music, relationships, youth culture and family themes.
  • Thattathin Marayathu and Hridayam became important contemporary youth-oriented Malayalam films.
  • He has repeatedly worked across acting, writing, music and filmmaking rather than staying within one role.
