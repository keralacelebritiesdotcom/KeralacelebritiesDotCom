About the Movie

Don't Trouble The Trouble is a 2026 fantasy comedy directed by Shashank Yeleti, starring Fahadh Faasil and child actor Ssara Palekar. The story follows Suri, a street magician who becomes caught up in the supernatural powers of a young girl after she mysteriously disappears during one of his tricks. The film is produced by Shobu Yarlagadda, Prasad Devineni and S.S. Karthikeya and presented by S.S. Rajamouli.
