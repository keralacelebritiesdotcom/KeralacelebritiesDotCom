Surya Krishnamurthy, theatre and cultural icon, passes away at 78

Veteran theatre personality, cultural organiser and former Kerala Sangeetha Nataka Akademi chairman Surya Krishnamurthy has passed away at the age of 78. He died at a hospital in Thiruvananthapuram after a period of illness. Founder of the Surya Stage and Film Society and the well-known Surya Festival, he was a major presence in Kerala's theatre and cultural life. He was also a former senior scientist at ISRO and received the Kerala Prabha award in 2023.
