Ahaana Krishna and Nimish Ravi announce their marriage

Published 16 Sep 2026By KeralaCelebrities.com

Malayalam actor Ahaana Krishna and cinematographer Nimish Ravi have shared their wedding news after getting married on September 13, 2026.

Malayalam actor Ahaana Krishna and cinematographer Nimish Ravi have announced that they are married. The couple shared wedding photographs on social media on September 15, confirming that the ceremony took place on September 13, 2026.

Nimish Ravi is known for his cinematography work, including the Malayalam film Lokah Chapter 1: Chandra. The wedding announcement formally confirmed a relationship that had attracted public attention in the weeks before the announcement.
